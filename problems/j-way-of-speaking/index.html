Maratona Mineira · Problem J

Way of Speaking

2025By Kaio Vieira, Bruno Monteiro, Gabriel Ubiratan

Statement

I feel your fury in your words, but I do not understand anything you say. — William Shakespeare

Bira traveled to the countryside of Minas Gerais and now needs to catch a train. When he arrives at the station, he asks an employee for the station number, to check whether he is at the right station.

However, the locals have a different custom: they say numbers in an encoded way! For example, to say the number 2020, they could say 220 (two copies of the number 20), and the number 205555 could be encoded as 12045 (one copy of the number 20, followed by four copies of the number 5).

Formally, a valid encoding of a number XXX consists of a number NNN whose digits can be partitioned into 2k2k2k parts (k≥1k\ge1k≥1), P1V1P2V2…PkVkP_1V_1P_2V_2\ldots P_kV_kP1​V1​P2​V2​…Pk​Vk​, such that XXX is the concatenation of P1P_1P1​ copies of V1V_1V1​, followed by P2P_2P2​ copies of V2V_2V2​, and so on. In other words, some parts of the spoken number are periods (numbers of repetitions) and repeat the digits that follow them. A period PiP_iPi​ may never begin with the digit 0.

When Bira asked for the station number, the employee said the encoded number NNN. There may be many ways to decode it. The number 321, for example, can be decoded as 212121 or as 11111111111111111111111111111111. Bira does not remember his station’s exact number, but he remembers that it had MMM digits.

Given the number NNN spoken by the employee, compute how many ways it can be decoded into a number with MMM digits. Two ways are different if they have different numbers of parts or if any part differs.

Input

The first line contains the number NNN (2≤∣N∣≤20002\le|N|\le20002≤∣N∣≤2000), where ∣N∣|N|∣N∣ is its number of digits. The first digit of NNN is not 0.

The second line contains MMM (1≤M≤20001\le M\le20001≤M≤2000).

Output

Print the number of ways to decode NNN into MMM digits, modulo 998244353998244353998244353.

Examples

Input
23225
6

Output
2

There are two ways to decode 23225 into 6 digits: 323255 and 332525.

3

Output

3

There are three ways, all producing 1111: [1](1)[1](111), [1](11)[1](11), and [1](111)[1](1), where brackets delimit periods and parentheses delimit repeated parts.

Tutorial

A valid encoding has a local substructure: after choosing P1,V1P_1,V_1P1​,V1​ whose decoding contributes M′M'M′ digits, the remaining suffix must decode to M−M′M-M'M−M′ digits. Use DP states (position in N, remaining decoded length).

Trying every split naively costs O(∣N∣2M)O(|N|^2M)O(∣N∣2M). For a tested period value XXX, the relevant transitions number O(M/X)O(M/X)O(M/X). Split at B=⌈M⌉B=\lceil\sqrt M\rceilB=⌈M​⌉: process X>BX>BX>B directly, while accumulating all transitions for X≤BX\le BX≤B in auxiliary tables indexed by the short period and target length. Prefix sums make each table update constant time.

This is the translated official approach. Its complexity is O(∣N∣MM)O(|N|M\sqrt M)O(∣N∣MM​).
